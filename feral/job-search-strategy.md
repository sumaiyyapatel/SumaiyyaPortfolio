## Track B — Target tiers (apply in this order, starting Day 6)

**Tier 1 — apply immediately, highest hit-rate.** Series A/B Indian product startups, design agencies, and smaller SaaS companies explicitly listing 0-2 years or "early career" — these will actually weigh your portfolio over your years. This is where volume makes sense: aim for 5-8 applications/week here, tailored but not agonized over.

**Tier 2 — apply, but budget less hope per application.** Roles like Adobe R170768 (2-4 years stated) — real shots, worth 1-2/week of your best, most-polished effort, but don't let the wait for a response here slow Tier 1 volume.

**Tier 3 — not yet.** Staff/Lead/Senior titles, anything stating 4+ years. Skip for now; revisit after 6-12 months of full-time experience.

## Track C — Outreach engine (start Day 6, runs in parallel with Track B)

You landed every freelance client through cold outreach — that's a real, proven skill, and it transfers directly to job hunting. Use it:
- Identify design leads/hiring managers at 10-15 target companies per week (LinkedIn, company team pages).
- Send a short, direct message referencing a specific thing about their product — not a generic "I'd love to work here." Attach or link 1-2 case studies max.
- This runs alongside, not instead of, formal applications — it's how you bypass ATS filtering on years-of-experience, since a human reading your portfolio directly can make a judgment call an ATS keyword filter won't.

## Weekly cadence

- **Mon:** review new postings across Tier 1/2, shortlist 8-10.
- **Tue-Wed:** send Tier 1 applications + 10-15 cold outreach messages.
- **Thu:** send 1-2 Tier 2 applications (highest polish).
- **Fri:** follow up on outreach from the prior week, update tracker.

## Parallel: close the interview-readiness gap

You've said you're not fully comfortable explaining your own code in depth — several of your case studies (Layer, Packr) claim hands-on frontend/dev ownership, and any real interview will probe this. Before your first live interview, do at least one practice run talking through your Layer and Packr code out loud, unscripted, including the AI-assisted parts (be ready to say plainly what Claude Code did vs. what you decided and directed — that's a legitimate and increasingly normal answer, but it needs to sound rehearsed-confident, not caught-off-guard).
